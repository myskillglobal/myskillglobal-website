🪖 Why a Helmet Matters

With HelmetFoam liner absorbs and spreads impact energy, cutting force to the skull dramatically.
Without HelmetFull impact force transfers directly to the skull and brain — even a low-speed fall can cause serious injury.
Rule of thumbA certified helmet can reduce head injury risk by more than half in a crash.

📋 Fall Speed vs Risk

20 km/hWithout helmet: bruising, possible concussion. With helmet: minor shock, usually safe.
40 km/hWithout helmet: high concussion/fracture risk. With helmet: reduced but still needs care.
60 km/hWithout helmet: life-threatening. With helmet: survivable, injury still possible — always ride within safe speed limits.
🪖 A helmet doesn't stop the accident — it stops the accident from taking your life.
