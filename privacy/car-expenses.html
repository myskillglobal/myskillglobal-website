MySkillGlobal – Drive Expenses Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Drive Expenses app, which helps users keep vehicle and expense records.

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Information you enter

The app may let you enter vehicle details, fuel entries, servicing records, taxes, fines, expenses, income, documents, donations, and related notes. Avoid entering unnecessary personal or financial information.

Purpose of use

Information you enter is used to provide the vehicle record, expense tracking, reporting, and related features you choose to use.

Storage and backups

Storage, synchronization, backup, and deletion behavior depends on the app version and enabled features. This policy does not claim that all information is stored locally or that no information is transmitted. Check the app's actual settings and disclosures.

Permissions and third-party services

If the app uses analytics, crash reporting, advertising, cloud storage, or other third-party services, those providers may receive technical or other data as described in their own policies. Review the permissions and disclosures presented in your installed version.

Advertising

If advertising is enabled in your version of the app, the advertising provider may process device or ad-related information under its own policies and applicable settings. Do not assume that advertising or analytics is absent unless the current app confirms this.

Data security and deletion

Use a device lock and keep your device and app updated. If the app provides delete or reset controls, use them to remove records you no longer need. Copies in backups or third-party services, if any, may follow separate retention rules.

Children's privacy

This app is a general vehicle-record utility and is not designed to solicit personal information from children. Parents or guardians can contact us with privacy concerns.

Policy changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. Please review this page periodically.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact: myskillglobal@gmail.com.
