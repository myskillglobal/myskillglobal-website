Car Expenses Manager – Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Car Expenses Manager Android app handles information when you use its vehicle and expense-record features.

Last updated: 14 November 2026

1. Information you enter

You may enter vehicle details and records such as fuel purchases, servicing, taxes, traffic fines, income, expenses, toll charges, documents, donations and related notes. Please do not enter information that is not needed for the app's features.

2. Storage of your records

The app's vehicle and expense-record screens are provided through an Android WebView using app content. Records entered into the app's local web storage are stored on the device where you use the app. The app does not provide user accounts or a developer-operated account dashboard for these records.

Do not assume that records are backed up or synchronized across devices. If you change devices, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app, locally stored records may be lost. Files that you select through Android's file picker may remain in their original device or provider location; their handling can depend on the Android version and the file provider.

3. Information processed by advertising services

This app integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to request and display banner and interstitial advertisements. When ads are requested or displayed, Google and its advertising partners may automatically process information such as:

  • Advertising ID or other device identifiers, subject to Android and user settings;
  • IP address, which may be used to infer approximate location;
  • Ad impressions, interactions and related app activity;
  • Diagnostics and information about app or SDK performance.

These data may be collected or shared by Google for advertising delivery and measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, security and compliance, as described in Google's policies and settings. MySkillGlobal does not use these advertising identifiers to identify you by your real-world name.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google's information about how Google uses data from apps.

4. Analytics and diagnostics

The Android project includes the Firebase Analytics library. Whether analytics events or automatic analytics collection are active depends on the app's runtime configuration and settings. If Firebase Analytics is enabled in the installed version, Google may process app activity, device or app information and diagnostics for analytics and service improvement in accordance with Firebase's privacy and security information.

The app's inclusion of an SDK does not mean that MySkillGlobal receives every item of information processed by that SDK. Please also review Google's privacy information linked above.

5. Permissions and selected files

The app uses internet access for advertising services. It also allows you to choose files through the Android file picker where the app offers that feature. Choosing a file does not, by itself, mean that the file is uploaded to MySkillGlobal. Do not select or share files unless you intend to use them with the app's available feature.

6. Data sharing

MySkillGlobal does not sell your locally entered vehicle or expense records. Information processed by Google Mobile Ads or, if enabled, Firebase Analytics may be transferred to Google and its service providers as described in their respective policies. MySkillGlobal does not operate a cloud account service for your vehicle and expense records in the app version covered by this policy.

7. Your choices and deleting information

You can remove locally stored app information by using any reset or delete controls provided inside the app. You can also clear the app's storage through your Android device settings (typically Settings → Apps → Car Expenses Manager → Storage → Clear storage/Clear data), or uninstall the app. Menu names may differ by device.

Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app removes local app data from that device, but does not itself delete information that Google may have processed under Google's services. For advertising and Google account privacy choices, use the relevant Google privacy and ad settings. This app does not currently provide a developer-hosted account deletion portal because it does not provide user accounts for these records.

8. Data security

Internet communications made by Google services are protected using the security measures described by those providers, including encryption in transit where supported. You should protect your device with a screen lock and keep Android and the app updated. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's privacy

Car Expenses Manager is a general vehicle-record utility and is not designed to solicit personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party service used by the app, please contact us and review the relevant provider's privacy policy.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, its features or its third-party services change. The updated date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision. Please review this page periodically.
