MySkillGame Privacy Policy

This page describes privacy considerations for MySkillGame, an educational game collection from MySkill Global.

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Information and game activity

MySkillGame provides educational games and learning activities. Depending on the features used, a learner may interact with quizzes, language exercises, stories, safety activities, or other educational content. Do not submit real-world sensitive personal information in free-text fields.

Offline use

Some MySkillGame activities are designed to work offline. Offline availability does not automatically mean every feature is offline; online features, links, analytics, or other services may behave differently. Check the current app and its permissions.

Children and parents

MySkillGame is intended for children and learners, so parents and guardians should guide use and review the app's available features. We do not intend this policy to authorize collection of children's personal information beyond what is disclosed by the app and permitted by law.

Advertising and third-party services

If the current version contains ads, analytics, speech services, or other third-party integrations, those providers may process data according to their own privacy policies. The information handled can vary by version, device settings, and the features enabled.

Permissions

Only grant permissions that are needed for a feature you choose to use. For example, voice input or audio features may require microphone or audio access if included in your installed version. You can review or change permissions in your device settings.

Security, retention, and deletion

Data handling and deletion depend on the specific feature and version. If you have a question about a record or believe personal information was submitted, contact us using the email below.

Policy changes

We may revise this policy as MySkillGame changes. The date at the top shows the latest published revision.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact: myskillglobal@gmail.com.
