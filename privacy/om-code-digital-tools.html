1. Introduction

OM Code & Digital Tools is a digital utility application developed by MySkill Global (Surajit Roy).

The application provides tools for creating and scanning OM Codes, generating and scanning QR Codes, and generating and scanning Barcodes.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be accessed, processed and handled when you use OM Code & Digital Tools.

Important: This Privacy Policy applies specifically to OM Code & Digital Tools. It is separate from the general Privacy Policy of the MySkill Global website.

2. Information You Provide

OM Code & Digital Tools is designed so that its core features can be used without creating an account or submitting personal information to the developer.

Depending on the feature you use, you may enter information such as:

  • Text
  • Numbers
  • Website URLs
  • Messages
  • QR Code, Barcode or OM Code content
  • Passwords for OM Codes
  • Other content that you choose to process

This content is processed on your device to create or read the code you requested. It is not sent to the developer and is not stored on our servers.

3. OM Code and Password-Protected Content

The OM Code feature allows users to create protected codes using a password.

The password is chosen by the user.

Users are responsible for keeping their passwords secure.

If a user forgets the password required to unlock a protected OM Code, the developer may not be able to recover or unlock the protected content.

Users should therefore keep their passwords in a safe place and should not share them with people they do not trust.

4. Camera Permission

The QR Code, Barcode and OM Code scanning features need access to the device camera. Camera access is used only when you choose a scanning feature.

The camera is used to:

  • Scan QR Codes
  • Scan Barcodes
  • Read information encoded in a visual code

Camera images are analysed on your device only to detect the code. They are not recorded, stored or sent to the developer or to any third party.

The application does not require camera access for creating a QR Code or generating a Barcode.

5. Photos and Images

If you choose to open an existing image from your gallery for scanning, the selected image is processed on your device to find and read a QR Code or Barcode.

The image is used only when you request it. It is not uploaded to the developer or to any third party.

6. Personal Information

We do not intentionally require users to create an account or submit personal information to use the core features of OM Code & Digital Tools.

If you voluntarily contact us by email or submit feedback, we may receive information that you provide in your message, such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Message content
  • Technical information included in your communication

This information may be used to respond to your communication, provide support, investigate an issue or improve the application.

7. Information Collected Automatically

The app includes Google services (Google AdMob and Google Analytics for Firebase). When you use the app, these services automatically collect certain information from your device and send it to Google:

  • Advertising ID, where available (you can reset or delete it in your Android settings)
  • App-instance ID / app set ID, a random identifier for this installation of the app
  • IP address, from which an approximate location (not precise location) may be derived
  • Device and app information, such as device model, Android version, app version and language
  • App interactions, such as app opened, a tool or page opened, a QR Code, Barcode or OM Code created or scanned, or a file saved
  • Diagnostic information, such as crash logs, performance data and app launch time

We do not collect precise location. The analytics events we record contain only the name of an action (for example, "qr_generate") and a general label such as the page name or code format. They never include the text you type, the content of codes you create or scan, passwords or your images.

The app displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob, including banner ads and full-screen (interstitial) ads.

Google may process the information described in Section 7 for purposes such as:

  • Delivering advertisements
  • Measuring advertising performance
  • Preventing advertising fraud
  • Limiting repeated advertisements
  • Personalising or non-personalising advertisements where applicable

Advertising behaviour may vary depending on your device, region, consent choices and advertising settings. You can learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

9. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob for advertising
  • Google Analytics for Firebase to understand which features are used and how the app performs
  • Google Play services and Android platform services

These services process information under their own privacy policies and terms. See the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information.

10. Data Storage and Processing

Creating and scanning QR Codes, Barcodes and OM Codes, including encrypting and unlocking OM Codes with a password, takes place on your device. The content, passwords and images you use for these features are not sent to the developer.

The information described in Section 7 is collected by Google services. Analytics data is kept in our Firebase project only as long as needed to understand app usage and improve the app, using Google's data retention settings.

We do not sell users' personal information.

We do not intentionally collect personal information merely because a user creates or scans a QR Code, Barcode or OM Code.

11. Children's Privacy

OM Code & Digital Tools is a general-purpose utility application. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

The application does not require anyone to create an account or provide personal information to use its core features.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

Users should also take reasonable precautions, particularly when creating and using password-protected OM Codes.

13. Data Retention
