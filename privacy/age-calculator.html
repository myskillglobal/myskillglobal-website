Age Calculator & Birth Chart Privacy Policy

This page explains privacy considerations for age calculation and, where available, birth-chart features.

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Information you enter

An age calculator may ask you to enter a date of birth or another date to calculate an age or date difference. Enter only dates you have permission to use and avoid sharing a person's date of birth publicly.

Calculation and storage

Calculation and storage behavior depends on the version and features provided. This policy does not promise that all calculations occur exclusively on your device or that entered dates are never transmitted. Check the current app's disclosures and permissions.

Birth chart features

If your version includes birth-chart or astrology features, you may enter a birth date, time, or place. These details can be personal information. Provide only what is necessary and review any in-app information about how it is handled.

Third-party services and advertising

If the app uses ads, analytics, crash reporting, or other third-party services, those providers may process technical or ad-related information under their own policies. The services used may vary by app version.

Children's privacy

The app is not intended to encourage children to disclose personal information. Parents and guardians should supervise use and contact us if they have a privacy concern.

Security and data deletion

Keep your device secure and review app permissions. If the app offers a way to clear saved information, use that option when appropriate. Any data held by third-party services may be subject to their own retention policies.

Policy changes

This policy may change as the app develops. Please review this page periodically.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact: myskillglobal@gmail.com.
