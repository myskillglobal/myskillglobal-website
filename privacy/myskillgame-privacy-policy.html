Privacy Policy

MySkillGame — Educational Game Collection
Last updated: 1 November 2026

1. Overview and Educational Scope

MySkillGame provides interactive educational games, quizzes, language learning modules, moral stories, and life-skills activities for children, learners, and families.

We prioritize user privacy and follow an offline-first architecture wherever possible. We do not require users or children to create accounts, provide passwords, or submit real-world personally identifiable information (such as full names, physical addresses, or phone numbers) to access the educational content.

2. Information Collection and Device Identifiers

To ensure app stability, detect performance issues, and support core functionality, certain technical data may be processed either directly or through verified third-party developer libraries (SDKs):

  • Device or Other Identifiers: Depending on device settings and the installed build, integrated third-party services (such as Google Play Services, Firebase Analytics, or Google Mobile Ads) may automatically process unique device identifiers. These may include the Android Advertising ID (AAID), Firebase Installation ID (FID), or anonymous app-instance identifiers.
  • Usage & Diagnostics Data: Anonymous crash logs, load times, feature interaction frequency, and device hardware specifications (e.g., OS version, device model) to diagnose technical bugs and enhance stability.
  • Local On-Device Storage: Game progress, quiz scores, and user preferences are stored strictly locally on the user's device and are not harvested or sent to external servers by MySkill Global.

3. Third-Party Services & Purpose of Processing

We partner with established platform services to support app distribution, reliability, and functionality. These third-party services process technical identifiers under their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services: Core app integrity, licensing, and update management.
  • Google Firebase Analytics: Aggregate, anonymous usage metrics and diagnostic monitoring.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Where advertisements are active, they are delivered through Google AdMob. In child-directed learning contexts, only non-personalized, contextual advertisements are served.

You can review Google’s comprehensive data handling and privacy standards here: Google Privacy & Terms.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Families Compliance)

Because MySkillGame is designed for children and learners, safeguarding child safety and privacy is paramount:

  • We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play’s Families Policy.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal contact information from children under 13 years of age.
  • We do not employ cross-app profiling, behavioral ad tracking, or interest-based targeted advertising for children.
  • Parents and guardians are encouraged to supervise their children’s digital device use and review installed app permissions.

5. App Permissions

MySkillGame only requests system permissions that are strictly necessary for specific, user-initiated features (such as audio/microphone access for interactive voice or phonics exercises, or local storage access for saving offline game state).

You can inspect, grant, or revoke permissions at any time via device settings: Settings > Apps > MySkillGame > Permissions.

6. Data Retention and Deletion Request

MySkill Global does not maintain remote user databases or collect personal user profiles. Technical diagnostic data handled by integrated third-party SDKs is automatically retained or purged according to standard third-party service lifecycles.

If you have questions regarding data handling or wish to submit an inquiry or request the deletion of any diagnostic records associated with your installation, please email us directly with the subject line "Data Inquiry / Deletion Request".

7. Policy Changes

We may periodically revise this Privacy Policy to reflect app updates, new educational activities, or evolving legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly to this URL, with the revised date indicated at the top of the page.
