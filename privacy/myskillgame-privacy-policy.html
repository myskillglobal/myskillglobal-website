MySkillGame Privacy Policy

This page describes privacy considerations for MySkillGame, an educational game collection from MySkill Global.

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Information and game activity

MySkillGame provides educational games and learning activities. The app does not require users to create an account, sign in, or register with the developer. The developer does not operate a user account system or maintain a user profile database for this app.

Do not enter real-world sensitive personal information into any free-text field that may be available in an activity.

Offline use

The educational games are designed to be usable offline. An internet connection may be used by third-party services, including advertising and analytics services. When the device is offline, advertisements may not load. Offline game use does not mean that third-party services are unable to process technical information when an internet connection is available.

Advertising services

MySkillGame uses Google AdMob to request and display advertisements when an internet connection is available. Google and its advertising partners may automatically process information such as device or advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from the IP address, ad interactions, and technical information needed to deliver, measure, and protect advertising services. The exact information processed may depend on the SDK version, device settings, consent choices, and applicable Google settings.

MySkill Global does not itself use this information to identify a learner by name. Advertising data handled by Google is subject to Google's policies and settings. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google advertising settings.

Analytics services

The app includes Firebase Analytics. This service may automatically process app-instance or device identifiers, app interactions and usage events, approximate location derived from technical information such as IP address, and diagnostic or technical information. These data help provide usage statistics and understand app performance. The information is processed by Google according to the Firebase and Google privacy terms and settings.

For more information, see Firebase privacy and security and Google's Privacy Policy.

Information stored by the developer

The app does not provide user accounts or a developer-operated online profile service. The developer does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, precise location, photos, or messages through the app's core offline games. The developer does not maintain a personal user database for MySkillGame. This statement does not mean that third-party advertising or analytics SDKs do not process the technical information described above.

Children and parents

MySkillGame is intended for children and learners. Parents and guardians are encouraged to guide children's use of the app. The app is intended to be operated in accordance with applicable Google Play Families requirements. Advertising and third-party SDK use must also follow the requirements that apply to the app's declared target audience.

We do not ask children to create an account or provide their name, email address, or phone number to use the core games.

Permissions

The Android app requests internet access so that online services such as advertisements can work. The AndroidManifest.xml supplied for this version declares the INTERNET permission and does not declare location, contacts, camera, or microphone permissions. Permissions may change in a future app version; please review the permissions shown by your device for the installed version.

Data sharing, retention, and deletion

Google AdMob and Firebase Analytics are third-party services. Information processed by those services may be transmitted to and retained by Google under the applicable service terms, privacy policies, and retention settings. MySkill Global does not control Google's independent retention systems and cannot directly delete information held by Google on a user's behalf.

For questions about information processed by Google services, please review Google's privacy resources and account or advertising controls. You may also contact us at the email address below; we will respond to questions about the app and information held by the developer.

Third-party links

The app or its learning content may link to third-party websites or services. Those services operate under their own privacy policies. MySkill Global does not control their content, security, or data practices. Please review their policies before using them or providing information.

Policy changes

We may update this policy when the app, its SDKs, or applicable requirements change. The date at the top shows the latest published revision. Please check this page periodically.

Contact

For questions about this policy or MySkillGame, contact: myskillglobal@gmail.com.
