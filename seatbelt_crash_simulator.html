✅ Why a Seat Belt Matters

With Seat BeltBelt locks and spreads crash force across the chest and hips, keeping you in your seat.
Without Seat BeltYour body keeps moving at crash speed and slams into the wheel, dashboard, or windscreen — or is thrown out.
Rule of thumbA seat belt cuts the risk of death in a crash by roughly half.

📋 Crash Speed vs Risk

20 km/hUnbelted: bruises, possible head hit. Belted: usually unharmed.
50 km/hUnbelted: severe injury likely. Belted: reduced but real injury risk.
80 km/hUnbelted: often fatal. Belted: serious but far more survivable.
🚗 The belt takes two seconds to fasten. A crash takes less than one to change your life.
