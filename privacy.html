Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

MySkill Global respects your privacy and aims to handle information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and handled when you visit or use the MySkill Global website and related online resources.

1. Information You Provide

You may choose to provide information when you contact us, send feedback, request support or use a feature that asks for information. This may include information such as your name, email address, message, feedback or other information that you voluntarily submit.

We do not ask users to provide personal information unless it is reasonably necessary for the feature or communication involved.

2. Website Usage and Technical Information

Like many websites, MySkill Global may receive standard technical information associated with visits to the website. Depending on the services enabled on a particular page, this may include information such as browser type, device information, approximate usage information, referring pages and technical data needed to operate and improve the website.

Analytics services may be used where enabled to understand how visitors use the website, identify technical problems and improve our educational resources and user experience.

3. Cookies and Advertising

MySkill Global may use cookies and similar technologies for website functionality, analytics and advertising.

If Google AdSense or other advertising services are enabled, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure advertisements on this website.

Google and its advertising partners may use advertising cookies to help serve ads based on a user's visits to this website or other websites. Depending on available settings and applicable requirements, advertising may be personalised or non-personalised.

Users can manage certain advertising preferences through Google's advertising settings. More information about how Google uses data when using partner sites and services is available from Google.

For information about Google's advertising cookies and related technologies, please refer to Google's official policies and help resources.

4. Third-Party Services and Links

Some pages or features of MySkill Global may use third-party services or contain links to external websites and applications. Examples may include analytics services, advertising services and separately hosted MySkill projects.

When you follow a link to a third-party website or service, that service may collect or process information according to its own privacy policy. MySkill Global does not control the privacy practices of independent third-party websites.

5. Educational Content and Children's Privacy

Some MySkill Global resources are educational and may be suitable for children and students. We aim to minimise unnecessary collection of personal information from children.

Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of online services where appropriate and should encourage children not to share unnecessary personal information online.

6. Feedback and Communication

If you voluntarily contact us by email or submit feedback, we may receive the information you provide in order to respond to your message, address your request, improve our services or understand user feedback.

7. Certificates and Assessments

Some MySkill Global assessments may provide certificates recognising achievement within the MySkill Global learning platform.

Where a certificate or verification feature requires information such as a name, certificate ID or assessment result, that information may be processed for the purpose of issuing, displaying or verifying the relevant achievement.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through the website. However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was provided, to respond to communications, maintain records, operate relevant features or meet applicable legal obligations.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in our website, services, technologies or applicable requirements. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
