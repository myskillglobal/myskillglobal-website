About the Platform

Building a Safer Future Through Road Safety Education

Every day, students, pedestrians, passengers and drivers face road risks. Awareness and responsible behaviour can help prevent accidents and save lives.

MySkillGlobal : Drive & Road Safety is an educational initiative designed to promote road safety awareness among children, students, families and everyday road users. It covers school bus safety, safe boarding and travelling, traffic rules, road signs, zebra crossings, helmet and seatbelt use, blind spot awareness, overspeeding dangers, emergency vehicle priority and accident prevention. Through interactive lessons, awareness tests, educational games and simulators, learners can develop practical safety knowledge. Our goal is to encourage responsible road behaviour and create safer communities. Available in Bengali, Hindi and English.

Why We Built This

Mission & Vision

Two important ideas guide every learning activity we create.

Our Mission

To educate children, students, drivers, passengers and communities about road safety through interactive learning, practical awareness activities and engaging educational games. We aim to promote safe travel habits, responsible driving and accident prevention.

Our Vision

A future where every child knows how to travel safely, every driver follows traffic rules, every passenger understands safety responsibilities and every road user respects the lives of others. We envision safer schools, safer journeys and safer roads for everyone.

Road Safety Awareness

Every Road User Has a Responsibility

Understanding everyday road risks is the first step towards safer journeys.

🚌 School Bus Safety

Children must follow safe boarding and travelling practices. Drivers, attendants and parents all have important roles in protecting students.

🪖 Helmet & Seatbelt Protection

Always use a suitable helmet and seatbelt. These protective measures can significantly reduce the risk of serious injury.

⚠️ Common Road Safety Risks

  • Overspeeding and reckless driving
  • Ignoring traffic signals and road signs
  • Not using helmets and seatbelts
  • Unsafe road crossing and pedestrian behaviour
  • Ignoring vehicle blind spots
  • Failing to give way to emergency vehicles

🚦 Safe Road Habits

  • Follow traffic rules and road signs
  • Use zebra crossings and pedestrian paths
  • Wear helmets and seatbelts correctly
  • Maintain a safe distance from vehicles
  • Stay alert near school buses and heavy vehicles
  • Give priority to emergency vehicles
This platform provides general educational and awareness information. Always follow the traffic laws and road safety regulations applicable in your country or region.
