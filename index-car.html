About the App

Built for real vehicle owners, not spreadsheets

A car or bike is one of the biggest recurring expenses in a household — yet most owners never actually see the full picture.

Car Expenses Manager lets you add every vehicle you own — car, bike, or scooter, petrol, diesel, CNG or EV — and log fuel fills, servicing, insurance, tolls, traffic fines, donations, and documents in one place. It quietly builds a 12-month income vs. expense summary, so the true cost of ownership is never a guess again. Built under the MySkillGame umbrella, it follows the same principles: works offline, collects no personal data, and never requires login.

Mission & Vision

Two ideas drive every feature we add.

Our Mission

To help every vehicle owner — from a first-time bike buyer to a family running two cars — see the true, honest cost of ownership, and make confident decisions about fuel, maintenance, and resale, without needing an accountant.

Car Ownership Awareness

Asset or Liability? Know before you decide

A few honest truths about owning a personal vehicle — the same awareness the app is built to support.

🚗 Asset or Liability?

Buying a car with a small down payment feels easy. The real question is: is it truly necessary, or a long-term liability?

📉 The Depreciation Truth

A personal car is not an investment — it loses roughly 10–15% of its value every year. Within 5 years, resale value can drop by almost half.

💸 Hidden Costs

  • EMI & loan interest
  • Fuel expenses
  • Servicing & maintenance
  • Insurance & taxes
  • Driver & parking

📈 Protecting Resale Value

  • Maintain a full service history
  • Avoid major accidents
  • Keep the car clean, use original spare parts
  • Keep insurance active, lower mileage = higher value
  • Best time to sell: 3–5 years
This awareness content is educational and general in nature — always evaluate your own finances before a major vehicle purchase.
