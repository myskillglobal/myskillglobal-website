The Shadow Hunter

A story of identity, deception, love and a new beginning

Set in the 1970s, this is the story of a helpless young man, the darkness of a criminal network, and the courage to stand on the side of truth. A Bengali mystery-social novel (Chhaya Shikari), in English.

বাংলায় পড়ুন

A young man about to end his life on the railway tracks is saved by a mysterious gentleman. But behind that help lies a terrifying web of human trafficking disguised as job offers. Which weighs more—gratitude, or the truth?

Author’s note: This is a work of fiction. Human trafficking and the illegal organ trade are serious crimes. The medical and investigative details in the story are part of the fiction and should not be taken as real events or as the identity of any real person. If you receive an offer of work abroad, verify the recruiter and employer through official channels, and report suspicious offers to your local police.

If you are struggling: The story touches on suicide. If you or someone you know is going through a difficult time, you can find free, confidential support in your country at findahelpline.com. In India, Tele-MANAS (14416) is a free government mental-health helpline.
