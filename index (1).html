MySkill Global

Learn · Play · Explore · Grow

MySkill Global is an independent learning platform bringing together educational games, language-learning resources, assessments, certificates, stories, practical life-skills content and useful digital tools in one place. Our aim is to make learning accessible, engaging and meaningful for students, families and communities, including learners who may not always have reliable internet access.

Our Story

What Is MySkill Global?

MySkill Global began as a single offline educational game created in India for students who often had limited or unreliable access to the internet. Over time, that project grew into a broader global learning platform bringing together educational games, language learning, assessments, certificates, stories and practical life-skills resources for learners around the world.

The platform is built around a simple idea: learning should be accessible and useful regardless of where a learner lives, how much internet access is available, or what type of device they use. Many of our resources, particularly selected educational games, are designed to support offline or low-connectivity use where practical. Some website features and online services require an internet connection.

Today, MySkill Global brings together MySkillGame, a growing collection of offline educational games covering areas such as safety awareness, science, general knowledge, personal finance and life skills. These activities are designed to encourage learning through interaction, practice, exploration and problem-solving.

The Language Learning section provides interactive learning resources and practical activities for people who want to develop their language skills. Depending on the resource, learners may find vocabulary, grammar, everyday expressions and other language-learning activities across different languages.

The Exam & Certificate section provides assessments that allow learners to practise, test their understanding and review their performance. Where an assessment is eligible for certification, participants who successfully meet the applicable requirements may receive a certificate recognising their achievement within the MySkill Global learning platform.

Certificates issued by MySkill Global recognise achievement within the platform. They are not government-issued certificates, university degrees, statutory qualifications or a replacement for qualifications issued by recognised educational institutions or authorities.

The Stories & Novels section provides reading materials for readers who enjoy stories and longer-form content. Selected resources may include comprehension questions or follow-up activities designed to encourage active reading and understanding.

Alongside its education-focused resources, MySkill Global also develops practical digital tools for everyday life. These include MySkill Drive & Expense, a tool designed to help drivers record and review vehicle-related expenses such as fuel, EV charging, tolls and other running costs.

AkashVoice is an independently developed concept exploring accessible communication and digital tools. The project considers ways technology could be made simpler and more accessible for students, senior citizens and communities where straightforward digital solutions may be useful. Features and availability may change as development continues.

MySkill Global is independently developed and maintained by Surajit Roy. Content, testing and educational review may also involve collaborators, including Sampurna Roy.

The platform is continuously developing, with new games, languages, assessments, stories and useful tools being added and improved over time. Feedback from students, teachers, families and other users helps us identify areas where the platform can become more useful, accessible and relevant.

Our broader goal is to use technology in a practical and responsible way to support knowledge, skills, creativity, awareness and lifelong learning. We believe that meaningful learning can take place both inside and outside the classroom, and that useful digital resources should be accessible to people from different backgrounds and communities.

MYSKILL APPS, GAMES & WEBSITES

Explore the growing collection of MySkill Global apps, educational games and practical digital tools created to support learning, everyday skills and responsible living. Each project is designed with a simple goal: make useful knowledge and technology easier to understand, access and use.

MyBudget is a personal expense-management project created to help users record everyday spending and develop a clearer view of their personal budget and financial habits.

More Small Story focuses on reading and short stories, encouraging storytelling, comprehension and a genuine interest in reading. Together, these projects represent different aspects of the MySkill Global vision — learning, practical skills, accessibility, financial awareness and everyday knowledge.

Our projects are independently developed and continue to evolve. New features, learning resources, games, guides and practical tools may be added over time. We welcome ideas and feedback from students, parents, teachers, drivers and everyday users who want to see useful digital resources grow.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is MySkill Global?

MySkill Global is an independent learning platform that brings together educational games, language-learning resources, assessments, certificates, stories, life-skills content and practical digital tools in one place.

Is MySkill Global free to use?

Many MySkill Global resources are available free of charge, including a range of educational games, learning materials, language resources and reading content. Availability may vary between individual resources, applications or services.

MySkillGame includes offline educational games that can be used without a continuous internet connection after the relevant content has been downloaded.

Who is MySkill Global designed for?

MySkill Global is designed for learners, students, teachers, families and communities around the world. Some resources are created especially for children and school students, while other resources are suitable for learners of different ages, backgrounds and levels of experience.

Do I need an internet connection to use MySkillGame?

Many MySkillGame activities are designed to work offline after they have been downloaded. However, some website features and online services may require an internet connection. For example, features such as online results, public leaderboards or web-based resources may require connectivity.

How do MySkill Global assessments work?

MySkill Global provides different quizzes, tests and assessments covering subjects and skills such as general knowledge, language, safety, life skills and other educational topics. Participants receive results according to the scoring system used by the relevant assessment.

Assessment format, scoring, eligibility and availability may vary between different assessments.

How do the certificates work?

Where an assessment is eligible for certification, participants who successfully meet the applicable requirements may receive a certificate recognising their achievement within the MySkill Global learning platform.

MySkill Global certificates represent achievement within the platform. They are not government-issued certificates, university degrees, statutory qualifications or a replacement for qualifications issued by recognised educational institutions or authorities.

Can I suggest a new game, language, story or feature?

Yes. We welcome constructive suggestions, corrections and ideas from learners, teachers, families and other users. You can use the feedback section or contact MySkill Global by email to share your suggestions.

Who develops and maintains MySkill Global?

MySkill Global is independently developed and maintained by Surajit Roy, a teacher and educational technology developer. Content, testing and educational input may also involve collaborators, including Sampurna Roy.

More information is available in the About the Developer section.

How can I contact MySkill Global?

For questions, suggestions, technical issues, content corrections, certificate-related enquiries or other matters, please contact us at myskillglobal@gmail.com .
