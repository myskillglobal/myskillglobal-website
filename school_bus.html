Stop 04 · Child Protection

Window Safety Features

School buses should be fitted with strong protective window safety nets or child-safe window guards where appropriate. These reduce the chance of children extending hands, heads, or objects outside the vehicle, while still allowing proper ventilation and visibility.

Net keeps hands in · child stays seated inside

🪑
Stop 05 · Interior

Child-Friendly Interior Design

  • Comfortable seats with secure seat belts, where available
  • Rounded interior edges to reduce injuries
  • Anti-slip flooring
  • Seat belt fastened
  • Window safety net in place
  • No standing while moving
  • No leaning out of the window
🚨
Stop 06 · Emergency

Emergency Evacuation System

In an emergency, every second matters. School buses should include:

  • Wide emergency doors and easy-to-open windows
  • Quick-release emergency exit mechanisms
  • Rescue hammer for breaking emergency glass
  • Regular evacuation drills for students and staff

Door → Children → Safe zone, in under a minute

👩‍🏫
Stop 07 · Education

Safety Education for Children

Children should learn:

  • How to board and leave the bus safely
  • Why they should remain seated while the bus is moving
  • Never extend hands or heads outside the window
  • How to respond during emergencies
  • The importance of listening to the driver and bus attendant
👨‍👩‍👧
Stop 08 · Parents

Parent Responsibilities

  • Ensure children arrive at the bus stop on time
  • Teach safe boarding and crossing practices
  • Inform the school about any medical conditions
  • Encourage children to follow bus safety rules
🏫
Stop 09 · School

School Responsibilities

  • Conduct regular safety inspections
  • Verify driver qualifications
  • Train bus attendants
  • Maintain emergency contact records
  • Organize periodic safety drills
  • Establish a clear incident reporting system
