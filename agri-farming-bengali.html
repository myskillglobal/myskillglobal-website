নতুনদের জন্য: ১০×১০ ফুট ঘর বা শেডে ওয়েস্টার মাশরুম দিয়ে শুরু করুন। ওয়েস্টার ২০–২৫°C তাপমাত্রা ও ৭০–৮০% আর্দ্রতায় জন্মায়। সাবস্ট্রেট হিসেবে ধানের খড় বা গমের খড় সংগ্রহ করুন — খামার থেকে বিনামূল্যে বা ₹১–৩/কেজি দরে পাওয়া যায়।
সাবস্ট্রেট (চাষের মাধ্যম) প্রস্তুত করুন
খড় ১২ ঘণ্টা জলে ভিজিয়ে রাখুন। প্রতিযোগী জীবাণু মারতে বাষ্পে জীবাণুমুক্ত করুন অথবা চুন (১% CaCO₃) দিয়ে শোধন করুন। স্পন দেওয়ার আগে ছড়িয়ে ঘরের তাপমাত্রায় ঠান্ডা করুন। এই ধাপই আপনার সাফল্যের হার নির্ধারণ করে।
স্পন সংগ্রহ ও প্রয়োগ
সরকারি ল্যাব বা বেসরকারি সরবরাহকারীর কাছ থেকে মাশরুম স্পন (₹৫০–২০০/কেজি) কিনুন। পলিথিন ব্যাগে সাবস্ট্রেট ও স্পন স্তরে স্তরে সাজান (৫:১ অনুপাত)। তুলার ফিল্টার দিয়ে ব্যাগ সিল করুন। ব্যাগ সাদা মাইসেলিয়ামে ঢেকে যাওয়া পর্যন্ত ১৫–২০ দিন অন্ধকার, পরিষ্কার ঘরে রাখুন।

শুকানো ও মূল্য সংযোজন

How to Start Ornamental Fish Farming
Set Up Basic Infrastructure
Start with 5–10 glass/fibreglass tanks of 50–100 litres each. Install air pumps, sponge filters, heaters (for tropical species). Shade cloth overhead to control temperature. Total setup cost: ₹15,000–50,000 for beginner unit.
Source Quality Brood Stock
Buy 2–3 pairs of healthy parent fish from trusted breeders in Kolkata's Galiff Street market or Howrah Maidan ornamental fish market. Choose species with high market demand and easy breeding — start with Guppy, Betta, or Goldfish.
Breeding & Fry Management
Condition breeding pairs with high-protein live food (Daphnia, Artemia, Bloodworm). Separate pregnant females to breeding tanks. Remove parents after spawning to protect fry. Feed fry with infusoria, then fine powdered food as they grow.
Grow-Out Phase
Transfer juveniles to larger grow-out tanks at 3–4 weeks. Grade fish by size — uniform size batches sell better. Keep tank water quality optimal: regular 20–30% water changes, temperature control. Good records help track breeding cycles.
Market & Export
Sell locally at Galiff Street, Howrah markets, or to pet shops. For export: register with MPEDA as ornamental fish exporter. Pack fish in oxygen-filled polythene bags with water; temperature-controlled boxes. Export via Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose International Airport, Kolkata.
🐠 Bishnupur / Bankura region: Freshwater ponds in this area are ideal for Koi Carp and Goldfish production. The proximity to Kolkata (150 km) means easy market access. The WBFDC ornamental fish training centre in Kolkata offers free 7-day training programmes — highly recommended.

Follow this 12-month plan to launch your Green Gold business — from selecting a crop to making your first export sale. Start small, grow steady.
12-Month Business Startup Roadmap
Month 1: Research & Choose Your Crop
Visit KVK Bishnupur to understand local soil, water and climate conditions. Attend any available demo session. Decide on ONE primary crop based on your space, investment capacity and local market demand. Visit successful local farmers before deciding.
Month 1–2: Register & Get Support
Register MSME Udyam (free, online). Open a current bank account. Apply for PMFME or MIDH scheme from district horticulture office. Join or form an SHG if needed. Visit NABARD district office for FPO information. FSSAI basic registration ₹100/year if making food products.
Month 2–3: Infrastructure & Training
Set up basic growing space: mushroom shed, fish tanks, or prepare land for dragon fruit/strawberry. Attend 5–7 day hands-on training at KVK or WBFDC. Source quality planting material/spawn/brood stock from certified sources. Do NOT use roadside vendors for seed/spawn — quality matters most.
Month 3–5: First Production Cycle
Complete first production cycle. Keep detailed records of input cost, yield, selling price. For mushroom: 3–4 flushes from first batch. For fish: first batch of fry to saleable size. Sell locally first — hotels, restaurants, pet shops, weekend haats. Reinvest 60–70% of profit into next cycle.
Month 5–7: Value Addition & Branding
Dry mushrooms, make jam from strawberries, process dragon fruit pulp. Create simple packaging with your farm name. Sell online via WhatsApp groups, local Facebook groups. List on Amazon/Meesho as a start. Feedback from customers helps improve quality for export standard.
Month 7–9: Quality Certification
Get IEC code from DGFT. Register with APEDA (for fruits/mushrooms) or MPEDA (for fish). Get product sample tested at NABL-accredited lab. Apply for HACCP documentation support from APEDA. Create product catalogue with lab reports — essential for export buyers.
Month 9–12: First Export Attempt
Create Alibaba / TradeIndia profile with photos, certifications, lab reports. Attend one virtual or physical trade fair (SIAL India, Aquarama online). Target trial shipment of 50–200 kg to one international buyer. Use APEDA's market development fund to offset sampling costs. Confirm first repeat order — that is your export business beginning!
