The Silent Assassin

Snake Bite Awareness · Common Krait · Timely Medical Care

Sourav was a bright eighth-grade student in a village. Every evening he would study, have dinner with his family, and fall asleep on his bed in their mud house.

It was monsoon season — bushes had grown thick and rats had become numerous. One night, a Common Krait snake entered the house searching for food and came close to sleeping Sourav. Suddenly, it bit him.

The astonishing thing was — Sourav felt almost no pain. He thought perhaps an insect had bitten him, and fell back asleep.

The Warning Signs Appear

Toward dawn, his speech became slurred. His eyelids grew heavy. Strength left his hands and feet. Breathing became difficult. The family at first thought he was simply unwell — they called the local faith healer. Precious time was lost.

By the time he was taken to the hospital, it was too late. Despite the doctors' best efforts, Sourav could not be saved. The entire village was silent with grief.

The Doctor's Awareness Session

A few days later, a health center doctor visited the village school and explained to everyone:

"Common Krait bites are often painless. That's why people don't realize. But it is extremely venomous. Drooping eyelids, slurred speech, difficulty swallowing, or difficulty breathing — if these symptoms appear, go to the hospital immediately without any delay. Wasting time with traditional healers can be fatal."

⚠️ Danger Signs — Go to Hospital Immediately

🔴Drooping eyelids or difficulty keeping eyes open
🔴Slurred speech or difficulty speaking
🔴Difficulty swallowing
🔴Weakness in arms and legs
🔴Difficulty breathing

Key Takeaways

🐍

Common Krait bites are often painless — do not ignore symptoms just because there is no pain.

🚑

After a snakebite, go to the nearest hospital as quickly as possible. Antivenom and treatment can save lives if given in time.

❌

Do not waste valuable time on traditional healers or home remedies. Do not cut the wound, suck the venom, or tie tightly.

🛏️

Use a mosquito net at night, keep your home clean, and reduce rats — this reduces the chance of snakes entering your home.

✅

Awareness and fast medical treatment are the most powerful tools against snakebite deaths.
