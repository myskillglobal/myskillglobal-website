Fish scale processing ranges from basic cleaning and drying to advanced collagen extraction. Choose the level that matches your investment capacity.
Step-by-Step Processing
Collection & Sorting
Collect fresh fish scales from fish markets, processing units, or own fishing catch. Sort by species — Rohu, Catla, Tilapia separately. Remove skin fragments and other debris by hand-sorting.
Washing & Deodorising
Wash thoroughly in fresh water 3–4 times. Soak in 5% NaCl (salt) solution for 2 hours to remove odour and surface bacteria. Then rinse with clean water and drain completely.
Pre-Treatment (Defatting)
Soak scales in a dilute acid solution (0.1M HCl or acetic acid) for 24–48 hours to remove mineral content and fats. This step is critical for collagen extraction quality. Rinse with distilled water after.
Drying
Sun-dry on clean mesh trays for 2–3 days OR use electric dryers at 40–50°C for 8–12 hours. Properly dried scales can be stored 6–12 months. Moisture content must be below 10% for export quality.
Collagen Extraction (Advanced)
Acid-soluble extraction method: dissolve pre-treated scales in 0.5M acetic acid at 4°C for 72 hours. Filter, precipitate with NaCl, centrifuge. Freeze-dry to obtain collagen powder. Yield: 15–30% of dry weight.
Powder / Granule Making
Dried scales can be ground in a hammer mill to fine powder (mesh 80–200). This fish scale powder is sold directly to fertiliser companies, feed manufacturers, and cosmetic ingredient suppliers at ₹60–120/kg.
Quality Testing & Packaging
Test for collagen purity (FTIR spectroscopy), heavy metals (arsenic, mercury, lead — all must be below EU/USFDA limits), and microbial count. Pack in food-grade, moisture-proof pouches with lot numbers for traceability.
Export-Ready Certification
Obtain EIC (Export Inspection Council) certificate. For EU markets: comply with REACH regulations. For USA: FDA registration required. ISO 22000 certification increases buyer confidence significantly.
⚠️ Basic drying and powder-making can be done with minimal investment (~₹50,000). Collagen extraction requires lab-grade equipment (~₹5–15 lakhs). Consider forming a cooperative or applying for government grants.

How to Apply — Quick Steps
Identify Your Scheme
Visit pmmsy.gov.in or your District Fisheries Office. Bring Aadhaar, bank account details, land/shop documents.
Prepare a DPR (Detailed Project Report)
Write a simple project report: what product, how much investment, how many jobs created, expected turnover. Templates available from NABARD district offices.
Bank Loan Application
Submit DPR + scheme application to any scheduled bank. PMEGP applications go through KVIC (Khadi and Village Industries Commission). Processing takes 2–3 months.
Get EDP Training
Most schemes require completion of a 2-week Entrepreneurship Development Programme (EDP). Available free through district DICs (District Industries Centres).

Export Requirements & Certifications
Mandatory Certifications
Before You Export
IEC (Importer-Exporter Code) — mandatory for all exporters, free from DGFT in 2–3 days online
EIC Certificate (Export Inspection Council) — required for fish and fish products export
HACCP (Hazard Analysis Critical Control Point) — food safety system, required by most buyers
ISO 22000:2018 — food safety management, preferred by EU and Japanese buyers
FSSAI registration — for any food-grade collagen or gelatin products exported
GMP certificate from State Drug Controller — for pharmaceutical-grade collagen export
Key Regulations to Know
Compliance
EU REACH regulation: all marine-derived ingredients must be registered and tested for safety
US FDA: registration required before exporting food/pharma products to the United States
Japan MHLW: strict heavy metal limits — mercury <0.5 ppm, lead <0.5 ppm in collagen products
CITES: ensure source fish species are not protected under wildlife trade agreements
GST: fish scale products (HS Code 0511.91) have 5% GST — input tax credit available

Follow this step-by-step roadmap to build a fish scale export business from scratch — from raw material collection to international shipping within 12–18 months.
12-Month Export Startup Roadmap
Month 1–2: Foundation
Visit local fish market to assess daily scale availability. Meet 3–5 fish dealers for supply agreements. Register business (MSME Udyam registration — free online). Open a current bank account. Obtain FSSAI basic registration (₹100/year).
Month 2–3: Raw Material Setup
Set up a clean collection and sorting area (can be a shed or open courtyard). Build or buy simple drying racks. Start collecting 50–100 kg of scales per week. Learn basic quality sorting — fresh vs stale scales. Contact NFDB or KVK for free technical guidance.
Month 3–4: First Product & Sales
Produce first batch of dried fish scales or scale powder. Sell locally to fertiliser dealers and feed companies at ₹20–40/kg. Use revenue to fund further investment. Apply for PMEGP loan if scaling up. Document all transactions for bank records.
Month 4–6: Quality & Certification
Get samples tested at NABL-accredited lab (CIFT, NIFTEM, or private labs). Apply for EIC registration and IEC code. Enroll in MPEDA's quality improvement scheme. Start HACCP documentation — templates available from EIC offices.
Month 6–9: First Export Attempt
Create an Alibaba/IndiaMART profile with lab reports and product photos. Connect with FIEO for buyer-seller introduction programs. Attend one trade fair (virtual options available for cost savings). Target small trial orders — 100–500 kg samples for verification by buyers.
Month 9–12: Scale Up
Secure 2–3 repeat international buyers. Invest in better equipment using first export revenues. Apply for collagen extraction equipment grant under PMMSY. Target ₹10–25 lakh annual export turnover in Year 1. Register with APEDA and MPEDA for government promotion support.
Year 2+: Expansion
Develop branded collagen powder product with own label. Apply for GI tag if using region-specific fish (e.g. Hilsa scales). Build an FPO with neighbouring fishing communities for supply chain. Target direct relationships with cosmetic companies, bypassing middlemen. Explore contract manufacturing for pharmaceutical companies.
