A Story About Time

This Life, Once Gone, Never Returns

A story of time, life, and consequence

The hands of the clock never stop. The time we imagine as spare simply slips away, unnoticed. Told across six chapters, this is the story of a life that pauses to ask — how much time did I truly have, and how much of it did I let slip through my fingers?

Start reading from Chapter 1

We often think, there's plenty of time left. But time waits for no one — this story is a journey toward that very realization.

Sources & Acknowledgements The philosophy of time at the heart of this story draws its original inspiration from a song written and directed by Tapan Sinha for the classic film Golpo Holeo Satyi (1966), and from the reflection on the passage of time found in Jogindranath Sarkar's poem Kakatua. Building on the ideas of that song, that poem, and this philosophy of time, the characters, events, dialogue, and story here have been independently created.
