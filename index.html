MySkill Global

Learn · Play · Explore · Grow

MySkillGlobal is an independent learning platform bringing together educational games, language-learning resources, assessments, certificates, stories, practical life-skills content and useful digital tools in one place. Our aim is to make learning accessible, engaging and meaningful for students, families and communities, including learners who may not always have reliable internet access.

Our Story

What Is MySkillGlobal?

MySkillGlobal is an independently developed learning and digital tools platform by Surajit Roy, bringing together educational games, practical apps, stories and skill-building resources for everyday life.

Explore MySkillGame for offline educational games, Language Learning and Global Typing Tutor for skill development, Exam & Certificate for assessments, and Stories & Novels for reading. Discover MySkill Drive & Expense for vehicle records, Age Calculator & Birth Chart for age and birth-chart information, QR Code & Barcode Reader and Writer for scanning and creating codes, OM Code Tool for password-protected information, Smart Lady for budgeting and savings, and AkashVoice for accessible communication concepts. MySkillGlobal continues to grow with a mission to promote knowledge, creativity, practical skills and lifelong learning.

🌱 Our Purpose — Learning Beyond the Classroom

The primary purpose of these stories and creative resources is to bring lost or lesser-known aspects of our culture, traditions and heritage to the attention of the younger generation.

Another important aim is to encourage children to develop self-confidence, human values, awareness, creativity and a positive connection with their cultural roots.

We also believe that learning is not limited to textbooks or classrooms. Stories, traditions, everyday experiences and the knowledge passed down through generations can also become meaningful sources of learning.

Our efforts therefore seek to connect cultural understanding with curiosity, creativity and practical learning, while presenting content in a simple and engaging form that younger readers can understand and enjoy.

  • To introduce younger readers to our cultural traditions and heritage.
  • To encourage self-confidence, creativity and human values among children.
  • To preserve and present cultural memories in a form that younger readers can enjoy and understand.
  • To encourage curiosity, meaningful learning and a positive connection between younger generations and their cultural roots.

Keeping children connected with healthy cultural values and meaningful learning is not always easy in a rapidly changing world. The author hopes that these efforts will contribute, in a small way, to that purpose.

Readers, parents, teachers and well-wishers are warmly encouraged to support and share this positive effort.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is MySkillGlobal?

MySkillGlobal is an independent learning platform that brings together educational games, language-learning resources, assessments, certificates, stories, life-skills content and practical digital tools in one place.

Is MySkillGlobal free to use?

Many MySkillGlobal resources are available free of charge, including a range of educational games, learning materials, language resources and reading content. Availability may vary between individual resources, applications or services.

MySkillGame includes offline educational games that can be used without a continuous internet connection after the relevant content has been downloaded.

Who is MySkillGlobal designed for?

MySkillGlobal is designed for learners, students, teachers, families and communities around the world. Some resources are created especially for children and school students, while other resources are suitable for learners of different ages, backgrounds and levels of experience.

Do I need an internet connection to use MySkillGame?

Many MySkillGame activities are designed to work offline after they have been downloaded. However, some website features and online services may require an internet connection. For example, features such as online results, public leaderboards or web-based resources may require connectivity.

How do MySkillGlobal assessments work?

MySkillGlobal provides different quizzes, tests and assessments covering subjects and skills such as general knowledge, language, safety, life skills and other educational topics. Participants receive results according to the scoring system used by the relevant assessment.

Assessment format, scoring, eligibility and availability may vary between different assessments.

How do the certificates work?

Where an assessment is eligible for certification, participants who successfully meet the applicable requirements may receive a certificate recognising their achievement within the MySkill Global learning platform.

MySkillGlobal certificates represent achievement within the platform. They are not government-issued certificates, university degrees, statutory qualifications or a replacement for qualifications issued by recognised educational institutions or authorities.

Can I suggest a new game, language, story or feature?

Yes. We welcome constructive suggestions, corrections and ideas from learners, teachers, families and other users. You can use the Contact page or email MySkillGlobal to share your suggestions.

Who develops and maintains MySkillGlobal?

MySkillGlobal is independently developed and maintained by Surajit Roy, a teacher and educational technology developer. Content, testing and educational input may also involve collaborators, including Sampurna Roy.

More information is available in the About the Developer section.

How can I contact MySkillGlobal?

For questions, suggestions, technical issues, content corrections, certificate-related enquiries or other matters, please contact us at myskillglobal@gmail.com .
