About the App

Basic Language Skills for Real-Life Situations

Learning a new language does not always have to begin with advanced grammar or long textbooks.

My Skill : Language focuses on the basic level — useful words, simple expressions, everyday communication and practical language knowledge. The platform is designed for learners who want to take their first step into another language and understand the basics before moving to a higher level.

It can be useful before travelling, studying, working, guiding tourists, or communicating with people from different linguistic backgrounds. Whether you are travelling to another country, preparing to study or work abroad, meeting international visitors, or working as a tourist guide, learning a few useful words in the local language can make communication easier and more comfortable.

Who Can Use It?

Built for Travellers, Students, Workers & Guides

Different people, different journeys — the same basic-level starting point.

🌍 Travellers & Tourists

Planning to visit another country? Learn some basic words and expressions before you travel. Knowing greetings, common questions, numbers, directions and everyday expressions can help you communicate with greater confidence.

🎓 Students

Planning to study in another country? A basic introduction to the local language can help students become familiar with common words and expressions before they begin their academic journey. This platform is a basic learning starting point, not a replacement for a full academic language course.

💼 Job Seekers & Workers

Going to another country for work? Basic language knowledge can make everyday communication easier — from greetings and simple conversations to common workplace situations. Learn the basics before you begin your journey.

🧭 Tourist Guides

Tourist guides regularly communicate with visitors from different countries. Learning basic expressions from multiple languages can help guides communicate with international visitors and provide a more welcoming experience.

Why We Built This

Mission & Vision

Two ideas drive every language section we add.

Our Mission

To make basic language learning simple, practical and accessible for people who want to travel, study, work, communicate or interact with people from different linguistic backgrounds. We aim to provide a simple starting point where learners can explore different languages without feeling overwhelmed by advanced grammar or complicated lessons.

Our Vision

A world where basic language knowledge helps people communicate across borders with greater confidence, respect and understanding. A traveller can prepare for a journey, a student can take a first step toward studying abroad, a worker can prepare for a new environment, and a tourist guide can develop useful multilingual communication skills.

Complete Your Learning Journey

Learn → Practice → Test → Certificate

Learning becomes more meaningful when you can check what you have learned. The journey is designed around four simple stages.

1

📖 Learn

Choose a language and explore its basic words, expressions and practical language knowledge.

2

🧠 Practice

Review what you have learned and become familiar with the language through practice.

3

📝 Test

After completing the basic learning section, take the available language test to check your understanding.

4

📜 Certificate

Learners who successfully complete the required test can download a PDF Certificate as a record of their basic-level learning achievement.

The certificate represents completion of the basic learning and assessment provided by My Skill : Language. It is not a professional language proficiency certification.

Important Note

Basic-Level Educational Learning

My Skill : Language is designed for basic-level educational learning. The lessons and tests are intended to provide introductory language knowledge.

They should not be considered a substitute for professional language courses, university-level language programs, employment language requirements, immigration requirements, or officially recognized language proficiency examinations. For academic admission, employment, immigration or professional certification, learners should follow the specific language requirements of the relevant institution or authority.
