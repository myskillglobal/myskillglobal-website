About MySkillGame

Learning Beyond Books • Skills for Life • Education for Everyone

Our Story

MySkillGame is an independent educational platform created to make learning engaging, practical, and meaningful for learners of all ages. Our mission is to combine education, life skills, technology, safety awareness, financial literacy, science, health, environmental responsibility, and vocational knowledge into one accessible platform. Today the platform contains more than 72 educational learning resources, along with interactive educational games, multilingual learning materials, practical guides, and awareness content.

What We Offer

🎮 Interactive Learning

Educational games designed to improve knowledge, thinking ability and practical understanding.

📚 Knowledge Library

More than 72 educational articles covering science, history, safety, finance, AI, languages and life skills.

🌍 Multilingual Learning

Learning materials in English, Bengali, Hindi and several international languages.

🛡 Safety Awareness

Road safety, disaster awareness, girls safety, health awareness and emergency preparedness.

💼 Career & Skills

Vocational education, entrepreneurship, employment awareness and practical life skills.

🤖 Future Technology

Artificial Intelligence, innovation, smart agriculture and emerging technologies.

About the Founder

👨

Surajit Roy

Surajit Roy is a Vocational Education Teacher from West Bengal, India and the founder of MySkillGame. With more than twenty years of experience in vocational education, he believes that education should go beyond textbooks and examinations. His vision is to prepare children and young learners for real-life challenges through practical knowledge, technology, awareness, and skill development.

Our Vision

To build a smarter, safer, healthier and more responsible generation by making quality educational resources freely accessible to everyone.
