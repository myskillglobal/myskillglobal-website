Our story

What Is MySkill Global?

MySkill Global began as a single offline educational game built for students in West Bengal who often had limited or unreliable access to the internet. Over time, that single project grew into a family of tools spanning education, language learning, exam preparation, and everyday life skills — all built around one idea: learning should not depend on where you live, how much data you can afford, or whether you have the latest device.

Today, MySkill Global brings together MySkillGame, a collection of over 75 offline educational games covering safety, science, personal finance, and life skills; a growing Language Learning section with interactive lessons and practical everyday phrases across multiple Indian and international languages; an Exam & Certificate section where learners can practice, test their understanding, and earn certificates to mark their progress; and a Stories & Novels library where readers can enjoy original and curated stories, then test their comprehension with follow-up questions.

Alongside the education-focused tools, MySkill Global also hosts practical apps for everyday life — including MySkill Drive & Expense, which helps drivers track fuel, EV charging, tolls and running costs, and AkashVoice, an early concept exploring offline micro-payments and accessible communication tools designed with students, senior citizens, and rural communities in mind.

Every product under the MySkill name is built and maintained by Surajit Roy, with content, testing, and educational review contributed by collaborators including Sampurna Roy. The platform is under continuous development, with new games, languages, and tools added regularly based on feedback from students, teachers, and everyday users — which is exactly why the feedback section below exists.

Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is MySkill Global free to use?

Yes. The games, language lessons, exam practice, and story library are all free to access. MySkillGame is also available as a free offline app on Google Play, so students without a steady internet connection can still learn.

Who is MySkill Global designed for?

The platform is built mainly for school students, young learners, and first-time job seekers in West Bengal and across India, but the language tools, exam practice, and story library are useful for learners of any age or background, including families and community groups.

Do I need an internet connection to use the games?

Most MySkillGame titles are designed to run offline once downloaded, which matters for learners in areas with limited or unreliable internet. Some features, like the public leaderboard and certain web-based tools, do require a connection.

How do the certificates work?

After completing an exam, language test, or story comprehension quiz, learners receive a score and a certificate marking that milestone. It's a simple way to track progress over time rather than a formal accreditation.

Can I suggest a new game, language, or feature?

Yes — the "We Would Like to Hear Your Opinion" section in the menu above is exactly for this. Feedback from students, teachers, and everyday users directly shapes what gets built next.

Who builds and maintains MySkill Global?

The platform is created and developed by Surajit Roy, with content review, storytelling, and educational input from collaborators including Sampurna Roy. See the "About the Developer" section below for more.
