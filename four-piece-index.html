The Four-Piece Treasure Map

A Hundred-Year-Old Truth Behind the Treasure

Introduction

An old ancestral house.
A mysterious letter.
And an incomplete map — every line of which seems to hold a secret a hundred years old.

When Kanchan's ancestral home catches the eye of the powerful businessman Arindam Chowdhury, strange events begin to unfold, one after another. The house's old walls, its well, its prayer room, and its Shiva lingam — everything in it seems entwined with a mystery no one can yet name.

Following clues left behind by his great-grandfather, Harinarayan, Kanchan discovers that the map is not complete. Its four missing pieces lie hidden in different corners of the very house he grew up in.

But with every piece recovered, the mystery only grows deeper.

In the darkness of the well, an unknown man waits.
Within the pages of an old manuscript lies an unexpected clue.
Beneath the Shiva lingam is found a mark whose meaning Kanchan cannot, at first, understand.

And into this dangerous search steps Supriya, standing beside Kanchan at every turn.

When the four pieces are finally brought together, they discover something unsettling — the map is not what it first appeared to be. An old mirror reveals to them its hidden, second form.

✦

Then — beneath the floor of the prayer room, a passage opens whose existence no one had ever known.

Deep within a dark underground chamber waits a truth a hundred years old. To reach it, Kanchan and Supriya must face a strange mechanical lock, a forgotten code, and a secret bound not only to their own family, but to the pasts of many others.

But what is Arindam Chowdhury's connection to all of this?
Why has he pursued this house so relentlessly, for so many years?
What was Harinarayan truly hiding?
And what waits inside the chest in that underground chamber?

Will the search for treasure lead only to gold — or will Kanchan come face to face with a truth whose worth no wealth can measure?

The Four-Piece Treasure Map is not simply a story about treasure. It is a thrilling journey through mystery, family history, love, greed, betrayal — and the search for truth.

Will the final chapter of a mystery that began a hundred years ago finally be written?
Where is the last piece of the map?
And what, in the end, is the real treasure?

The answer lies waiting in the darkness of the underground chamber...
